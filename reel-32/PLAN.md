## The chain

The one object is **a skill**: one card that tries to be everything, splits into one per
format, is trained on her content, and ends as her three skills fed by what worked.

near: consultation call, the client's tile speaks → her speech bubble becomes Claude's text,
half struck and rewritten → a magnifier reads it, it flips into ONE skill («1») → four format
chips plug in → each takes its own style, cables turn to sparks, the chips are crammed into the
card → it prints a muddled text: fish ✕, meat ✕ → the card splits into four, each gets its
format → the Reels skill rises, a prompt types a description of her style and deletes it →
**OVER**: iris out of «send»: a wall of her content, run, a scan reads it; МЕТОДИ / СТИЛЬ /
МАНЕРА СПІЛКУВАННЯ pull out; they fold into a sheet «Правила» (1–5) → iris closes onto the
test text: it writes, she reads it with a highlighter, «Окей», the lines take her colour,
Claude + her ✓ → near: a shelf of her three skills, ghost → lit (Клієнти, Контент,
Лідмагніти) → her best posts (hearts) feed them; two random cards and a die are thrown in,
crossed out → Стежити.
