## The chain

«під ключ» behind her → the answer to «що мені треба» is a **checklist «Від тебе»**
of five ghost rows that fills in, row by row, on each thing she names → its last
row (rules for scripts) opens into a card: a **voice note** that goes into the
**скіли** → the panel: the one thing the client writes, the core **«про тебе»**
(ти), gets **structure** and a **place in the system**, and every other part of
that system is hers (я) → what is left for the client: look at the **result**
and press **окей / не окей** → CTA.
