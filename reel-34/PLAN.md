## The chain

One repeating mechanic that escalates: **a Claude dock collects the right
tools.** She points up-left on every «Для X» and up-right on every answer, so
the wrong tool lands on a dark-glass card on the LEFT, the right one on a white
card on the RIGHT, and on «в Клод» the right tool's tile flies up into the dock
beside the Claude hub. Lines 1–2 strike the wrong card on «ні» and knock it
off; lines 3–6 have no «ні», so the right card shoves the wrong one off as it
arrives (faster, harder). The hub grows a little with every tool it eats.

near (dock + six empty slots) → six swaps, the dock fills centre-out → the sixth
tile lands and the **iris opens out of the hub** → OVER: the six wrong
platforms scattered, a «you» puck hops tile to tile on «стрибати між купою»,
a € lands on each on «платити за кожну» → all sucked into one big Claude tile on
«взяти одну платформу» → it rises onto a white sheet: the six right tools, their
toggles switch on along «налаштувати всю систему», a «you» badge lands on
«під тебе» → **iris closes onto the hub** → near: the full dock is back («так
само») → an Instagram comment field types «+» and sends.
