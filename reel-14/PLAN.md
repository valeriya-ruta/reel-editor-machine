## The chain

This is a product reel, so the language is **an Apple product page**: spec
callouts on hairline leaders with a dot at the tip, pointing at the thing in her
hand — «0,5 кг», «150 см», «8 €», «MagSafe», «3 000 ₴». Numbers only, as she
says them. Leader positions come off her frames at build time.

The one object is **the bag**: a glass packing list that gains a line and a
check (✓ + rule, her checklist rule) as each item is named, and is complete on
«І це все». It is the list shown bare early (two rules, nothing packed) and full
at the end.

The small phone holder is the one item she cannot show — it is holding the
camera. That beat is the reel's takeover: her photos of it, cut out, on the
burgundy ground.
