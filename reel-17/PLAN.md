Posts 2026-10-01 · ~71 s (212 words) · variant B · refined glass (STYLE.md §0)

## The chain

A reel about AI-edited reels, edited by AI — so it gets to be meta. The one
object is **her profile grid**. Bare early: a month-long gap, reels stuck in
drafts. Paid off: the grid full, and on «ось результат» **this very reel shrinks
into one cell of that grid** — the plate itself scales down into a tile while
her other reels are around it.

The contrast in the middle is the thesis made visible: a deliberately garish
"AI-style" frame (glow, rainbow, stickers — the one sanctioned off-palette frame)
that gets *cleaned* in place into the refined glass look.
